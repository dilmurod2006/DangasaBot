conventional 
  adj  /kənˈvenʃənl/
Conventional exercise routines, such as jogging and cycling, 
are still very popular. 
devise  v  /dɪˈvaɪz/
Psychologist Kurt Lewin devised a three-step model for the 
effective management of change. 
dispute 
  v  /dɪˈspjuːt/
Some recent research would appear to dispute these claims.
eligible  adj  /ˈelɪdʒəbl/
At what age are you eligible to vote in your country?
expertise 
  n  /ˌekspɜːˈtiːz/
Anyone who wants to implement change must first ensure 
that their knowledge and expertise is recognized.
expressway  n  /ɪkˈspresweɪ/
They always seem to be building a new expressway or 
widening an existing one. 
facilitate  v  /fəˈsɪlɪteɪt/
In Germany, change can be facilitated by formal written 
communication. 
fad  n  /fæd/
The new fad is for High-Intensity Interval Training. 
fairy-tale  adj  /ˈfeəri ˌteɪl/
A solitary green ogre finds his life is interrupted by various 
fairy-tale characters. 
feisty  adj  /ˈfaɪsti/
The feisty heroine is a schoolgirl who becomes a zombie.
formal 
  adj  /ˈfɔːml/
Everyone is dressed in formal clothes, and you’re in jeans and 
a T-shirt.
formally 
  adv  /ˈfɔːməli/
Communication of change should also be done formally, 
through written documents.
fuss  n  /fʌs/
I never make a fuss in restaurants, even if they bring me the 
wrong food.
heroine  n  /ˈherəʊɪn/
The heroine is an ordinary school teacher who has special 
powers. 
implement 
  v  /ˈɪmplɪmənt/
Those wishing to implement change in Anglo-Saxon countries 
need to highlight benefits to the team.
impose (on) 
  v  /ɪmˈpəʊz ˌɒn/
It’s best to agree change with your team, rather than impose it 
on them. 
in the region of 
  phr  /ɪn ðə ˈriːdʒən ɒv/
They‘re planning to build somewhere in the region of seven 
more metro lines. 
infinitely  adv  /ˈɪnfɪnətli/
We now expect everything to be available infinitely more 
quickly than in the past.
intense 
  adj  /ɪnˈtens/
It’s thought that intense physical activity is better for you than 
gentler exercise. 
intensity 
  n  /ɪnˈtensəti/
Low-intensity exercise is less effective at burning fat than high-
intensity exercise. 
level (off)  v  /ˌlevl ˈɒf/
The unemployment rate has levelled off over the last couple of 
years. 
maintain 
  v  /meɪnˈteɪn/
They also maintain that it makes you burn more calories during 
the day. 
make your day  phr  /ˌmeɪk jɔː(r) ˈdeɪ/
It made my day when my colleague complimented me on my 
presentation.
metabolism  n  /məˈtæbəlɪzəm/
They maintain that it speeds up metabolism. 
metamorphosis  n  /ˌmetəˈmɔːfəsɪs/
There are various reasons why the idea of metamorphosis is 
so important in art and culture.

  n  /ˈmɒdl/

  n  /ˌməʊtɪˈveɪʃn/

  n  /mjuːˈteɪʃn/

nerdy  adj  /ˈnɜːdi/

first sign of danger. 
nostalgia  n  /nɒˈstældʒə/

by a sense of nostalgia. 
nutritious  adj  /njuˈtrɪʃəs/

In some countries, people believe that the person at the top of 
the company has a good overview.

pick up on  phr v  /ˌpɪk ˈʌp ɒn/

prestigious  adj  /preˈstɪdʒəs/

changed into somebody else. 
protagonist  n  /prəˈtæɡənɪst/
The protagonist is a young boy who is brought up by his elder 
sister and her husband. 
rate 
  n  /reɪt/
Big cities like Delhi and Mumbai are growing at a much slower 
rate. 
resist 
  v  /rɪˈzɪst/

resistant  adj  /rɪˈzɪstənt/
If managers fail to take these factors into account, they may

revolutionize  v  /ˌrevəˈluːʃənaɪz/

principles. 
scenario  n  /səˈnɑːriəʊ/

our society. 
short and sharp  phr  /ˌʃɔːt ænd ˈʃɑːp/
Get on that rowing machine and keep it short and sharp!
skinny  adj  /ˈskɪni/
He was really skinny after a month of trekking in the jungle.
soar  v  /sɔː(r)/

closed. 
stabilize  v  /ˈsteɪbəlaɪz/
Global population growth is showing no signs of stabilizing. 
symptomatic  adj  /ˌsɪmptəˈmætɪk/
It could be claimed that these tales are symptomatic of our age.
