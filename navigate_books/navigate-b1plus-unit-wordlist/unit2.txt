  v  /əˈpɪə(r)/

  n  /əˈtempt/

  v  /bɪˈliːv/

  n  /breθ/

  n  /ˈkeɪbl kɑː(r)/

  v  /tʃɑːdʒ/
A hoax story claimed that dialling 999 would charge your 
phone battery.
circulate  v  /ˈsɜːkjəleɪt/
Recently, a picture has been circulating on the internet of a 
bright blue watermelon.
claim 
  v  /kleɪm/
The hoax story claims that calling 999 will charge your phone 
battery.
coincidence  n  /kəʊˈɪnsɪdəns/
Coincidences are often meaningful and ‘meant to happen’.
conclusion 
  n  /kənˈkluːʒn/

they could hear, but what they could see.

Expert: a man who makes three correct guesses consecutively.
create 
  v  /kriˈeɪt/

photos and videos digitally.
curiously  adv  /ˈkjʊəriəsli/

centre.
disappear 
  v  /ˌdɪsəˈpɪə(r)/

escape 
  n  /ɪˈskeɪp/
That reminds me of another story I heard about a man who 
had a lucky escape.
evidence 
  n  /ˈevɪdəns/
Scientists were keen to find some evidence that would prove 
the link.
expect 
  v  /ɪkˈspekt/
Did you expect that to happen?
fake  n  /feɪk/
In 1953 a team of researchers discovered that it was, in fact, 
a fake.
float 
  v  /fləʊt/
On October 15, 2009, the media reported that a six-year-old 
boy was inside a large silver balloon floating high in the sky.
fortunately  adv  /ˈfɔːtʃənətli/
Fortunately, we got to the airport on time.
free 
  v  /friː/
I managed to free one hand and felt around – my hand touched 
a hippo’s nose.
hire 
  v  /ˈhaɪə(r)/
Orchestras started hiring many more women.
hoax  n  /həʊks/
It turns out the story was another internet hoax.
inform 
  v  /ɪnˈfɔːm/
The police were informed.
interestingly  adv  /ˈɪntrəstɪŋli/
Interestingly, I know a lot of people who want to work on 
television.
